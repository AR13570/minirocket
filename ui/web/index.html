Three additions to MiniRocket

Each one is a separate module that reuses the original transform and the faster joint-PPV version, without changing them.

Multi-pooling + difference series

MiniRocket counts how often a pattern appears. Multi-pooling also measures how strongly, where and for how long, on the raw series and on its first difference. Each dataset keeps the extra feature only when its own training data shows a gain.

How the results were kept honest

Every choice the models make, such as adding MPV or where to stop early, uses training data only. The design was fixed on 10 datasets and then checked on 20 datasets downloaded afterwards.

Ideas that did not help are reported too: MIPV, LSPV and the difference series lowered accuracy in the ablation, so the final model does not use them by default.
